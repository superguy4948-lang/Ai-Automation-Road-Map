01 — Foundation

What is AI Automation?

🤖

AI Automation

AI Automation means using artificial intelligence to perform tasks automatically — without you doing them manually. Think of it like training a robot to do your boring, repetitive work 24/7 while you sleep.

🧠

AI Agents

AI Agents are smarter — they can think, decide, and take actions on their own to complete a goal. They're like an AI employee who reads your email, decides what to do, and gets it done — without you asking step by step.

⚙️

n8n — Your Automation Superpower

n8n is a free, open-source tool that lets you visually connect apps and AI together using a drag-and-drop workflow builder. It's like Zapier — but 10x more powerful and you can self-host it for FREE.

🔗

How They Work Together

You build a workflow in n8n, plug in an AI model (like GPT or Gemini), and your system can now automatically reply to messages, generate content, scrape data, send reports — all on autopilot.

03 — The Path

Your Learning Roadmap

01
Understand the Basics
⏱ Week 1–2
Learn what APIs are APIs are how apps talk to each other. This is the backbone of all automations.
Understand webhooks & triggers Learn how automations get "triggered" when something happens (like a new email).
Explore n8n Cloud (free tier) Sign up at n8n.io and play with the drag-and-drop interface. Don't overthink it.
Build your first basic workflow Simple task: When I get a Gmail, send me a Telegram notification.
02
n8n Core Skills
⏱ Week 3–5
Master key n8n nodes HTTP Request, Set, If, Switch, Merge, Loop Over Items — these are your building blocks.
Connect Google Sheets + Gmail Build a workflow that reads data from a spreadsheet and sends automated emails.
Learn JSON & data transformation Understand how to manipulate data as it flows through your workflows.
Self-host n8n on Railway (free) Move to self-hosting for unlimited workflows without cost limits.
03
Add AI to Workflows
⏱ Week 6–8
Connect free AI APIs Use Google Gemini (free), Groq (free with llama-3.3-70b), or OpenRouter.
Build an AI content workflow Auto-generate Instagram captions or blog posts using AI + Google Sheets.
Create a customer support bot Build a chatbot that reads your FAQ and auto-replies to messages.
Understand prompting for automations Learn how to write system prompts that make AI behave reliably in workflows.
04
Build Real AI Agents
⏱ Week 9–12
Learn n8n's AI Agent node n8n has a built-in AI Agent node — learn how to give it tools and memory.
Add memory with Postgres Give your AI agent conversation memory so it remembers context between messages.
Build a WhatsApp AI Agent Connect Meta WhatsApp Business API + AI to reply to customers automatically.
Add web search tools to agent Give your agent the ability to search the internet and use results in replies.
05
Monetize & Go Pro
⏱ Month 4+
Build a portfolio of 3–5 projects Document your workflows with screenshots and short demo videos on YouTube/Instagram.
Find clients on Upwork & LinkedIn Search "Zapier automation", "Make.com", "workflow automation" — those are your potential clients.
Offer productized services Package your automations as fixed-price offers: "WhatsApp AI Bot — $299 setup".
Build & sell n8n templates Share workflows on n8n's community hub — build reputation & passive income.

How to Learn Fast

🎯
Build, Don't Just Watch
For every video you watch, immediately try to replicate what they built. Passive watching = zero skills. Active building = real skills.
📱
Document Your Journey
Post what you build on Instagram or YouTube. Your audience grows, your skills solidify, and you attract clients — all at the same time.
⚡
1 Hour Daily = Enough
Consistency beats intensity. 1 focused hour every day for 3 months will make you more skilled than someone who studies 5 hours once a week.

Errors are your best teachers. When something breaks, don't give up — debug it. That problem-solving skill is what clients pay for.
